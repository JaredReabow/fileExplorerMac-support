Privacy Policy

fileExplorerMac is a local macOS file browser.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Information we collect

fileExplorerMac does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, telemetry, or third-party crash reporting.

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information through the app.

Files and folders

Files, folder names, paths, previews, search results, and metadata are processed on your Mac to display and operate on the locations you choose. The developer does not receive that app data. Access outside the app container is limited by macOS App Sandbox permissions and by folders or locations you explicitly choose.

If you browse or act on a network or cloud-backed location, your Mac or its sync client may exchange files and metadata with that provider so the requested view or file operation can complete. The provider handles that exchange under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive a copy of that provider exchange.

Saved settings and access choices

Preferences and security-scoped bookmarks are stored locally in the app’s container on your Mac. They are used to remember choices such as your last folder, view settings, and folders you asked the app to reopen. The app reads a saved grant only when needed to open a remembered location. Closing the app ends its active access; it does not delete the saved grant.

Saved preferences remain until you change them in the app or remove the app’s container data. Saved bookmarks remain until the container data is removed or a bookmark becomes unusable and the app drops it; this build does not provide a separate control for removing an individual saved bookmark. Removing the app from Applications alone does not necessarily remove that container data. To delete saved grants and settings, remove the app’s container through your macOS data-management controls or an equivalent local cleanup tool. After the container data is removed, the app cannot reuse those saved grants; macOS will require you to choose the location again.

Support requests

Support is provided through the public GitHub issue tracker. Information you choose to post there is handled by GitHub under its terms and privacy policy. Do not post passwords, private file contents, or other sensitive information.

Children

The app is a general-purpose file browser and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone through the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new last-updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public issue tracker.
